# Change notes

Versioned changes, internal build numbers, APK checksums, and verification notes are published on [Releases](https://github.com/toyo-craft/ttdraw/releases). Pre-publication technical candidates are not represented as publicly distributed releases.

## Early distribution features

- Vector sketches with selection, transformations, alignment, and grouping.
- Foreground/background layers, image/SVG import, and templates.
- PNG saving, sharing, and copying; TTD transfer of saved data.
- Japanese/English information screens, adopted Terms and Privacy Policy, public reports, and private inquiries.

Fixes are grouped into new versions and build numbers while retaining previous public release history. Find the recommended latest version on Releases.
