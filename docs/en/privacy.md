# TTDraw Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Toyo Craft lab (the “Provider”) establishes this policy for the handling of information in the current free version of TTDraw.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to the TTDraw Android app and the verification Web version provided from the same Flutter codebase. The current version does not include login to a Provider account, saving or synchronization of sketches to a Provider server, or in-app contracts or payments.

## 2. Information stored on the device or in the browser

The App stores on the device the information needed to provide its functions, including sketch content, text, imported images, templates, names, favorites, save dates, language, and drawing settings. The Web version uses the storage area of the browser in use.

The App has no function that automatically sends this drawing content or file names to the Provider’s server. The Web version obtains the app files, fonts, drawing resources, and version information from its distribution source. During those communications, the distribution source or its infrastructure provider may handle IP addresses, dates and times, and similar information normally associated with access.

## 3. Image import, output, and sharing

Images and TTDraw files selected by the user are read and processed on the device or in the browser for drawing, editing, and saving.

When the user chooses to save, share, copy to the clipboard, or export, the relevant data is passed to the selected destination, sharing app, or OS or browser clipboard. The terms of each destination or service apply to its handling of that data. Exported files and images may remain at their destination after the App is removed.

## 4. Permissions and device functions

The App uses OS or browser functions needed for file selection, image saving, sharing, and clipboard operations. On older Android versions, storage permission may be requested to save images. Users can manage permissions in OS or browser settings, but the corresponding function may be unavailable when permission is not granted.

## 5. Advertising and analytics

The current version does not include an advertising SDK or an analytics SDK that sends usage behavior to the Provider. It has no function that automatically sends sketch content, entered text, file names, or operation history to the Provider for analysis.

Statistics, diagnostics, backups, or similar functions independently provided by Google Play, an OS, or a browser are governed by the policy of that provider and the user’s settings. Those platform functions are separate from analytics performed by the App itself.

## 6. Retention, deletion, and backup

Data stored on the device or in the browser is retained until it is deleted by the user, automatically cleaned up under the App’s storage conditions, or removed by the OS or browser. The App displays the regular-sketch limit and automatic-cleanup conditions, together with excluded items such as favorites and templates.

Saved data can be removed through deletion in the App or by clearing app data in the OS or browser. Uninstalling the Android app normally removes its on-device app data, but does not guarantee simultaneous deletion of OS backups or copies at other destinations. Web-version data can also be deleted through the browser’s site-data settings.

Depending on OS settings and services, app data may be included in OS-provided backup or device migration. This is not a cloud-storage service provided by the Provider. Back up important data separately by export or another method, and manage unneeded exported or backed-up data at each destination.

## 7. External websites and inquiries

When the user opens the official website, the App moves to that site in an external browser. Handling of information at the destination is governed by that site’s privacy policy.

When the Provider receives an inquiry, it uses the contact details and inquiry content sent by the user only as needed to respond. The user decides whether to attach sketches or other data. Do not attach unnecessary personal or confidential information.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If the App’s functions or handling of information changes, this policy will be updated as necessary. Important changes and their effective date will be announced in the App or on the official website, and consent will be obtained where required by law. If accounts, paid-contract integration, cloud storage, analytics, or similar functions are introduced in the future, their actual handling will be reflected before they are provided.

## 9. Provider and contact point

Provider: Toyo Craft lab.

Public reports (bugs and requests): https://github.com/toyo-craft/ttdraw/issues

Private inquiries (including personal or confidential information): info@toyo-craft.net

Do not include personal or confidential information in public reports. Use the private inquiry email address for information that cannot be made public.
